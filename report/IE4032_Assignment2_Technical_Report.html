1. Scope, Threat Landscape & Methodological Limitations

On 12 October 2026, AsteriaGrid—a critical utility and infrastructure operator—experienced intermittent public portal disruptions. Within 20 minutes, an aggressive online campaign alleged that AsteriaGrid's SCADA networks had suffered a catastrophic adversary attack, compromising industrial control systems and exposing 25,000 customer records. This investigation deconstructs narrative development, validates digital evidence, reconstructs propagation networks, and formulates a defensive response.

Methodological & Data Caveats
  • Synthetic Simulation: All entities, accounts (Account_01–Account_40), and URLs (example.invalid) are synthetic assessment artifacts. No external infrastructure was probed.
  • Dataset Bounds: Chronology is bounded by the 120 synthetic posts, 10 registered sources, and 8 media artifacts.
  • Network Metrics Interpretation: Centrality measures describe mathematical graph position within the citation model; they do not prove real-world psychological persuasion, reach, or off-platform impact.

2. Analytical Methodology & Investigation Workflow

The investigation implemented Intelligence Community Directive (ICD) 203 standards, combining open-source intelligence (OSINT) verification workflows with quantitative social network analysis (SNA):

  1. Cryptographic Ingestion: Normalized data schema and computed SHA-256 digests across raw CSVs and evidence registers.
  2. Chronological Normalization: Normalized all event timestamps to Universal Time Coordinated (UTC).
  3. Directed Graph Modeling: Formalized directed edge e = (u → v) where parent post u is cited, retweeted, or replied to by child post v. Calculated Degree Centrality, Betweenness Centrality (Brandes' Algorithm), and PageRank (α = 0.85).
  4. Linguistic Text Clustering: Grouped text across the corpus, discovering that 120 posts derive from only 14 distinct templates.
  5. Media Forensics: Executed reverse archival searches, Error Level Analysis (ELA), and schema pattern audits on artifacts M01–M08.
