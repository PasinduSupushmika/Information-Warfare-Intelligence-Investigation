INFORMATION WARFARE INVESTIGATION DATASET
All organizations, accounts, URLs, and events in this dataset are fictional and created for academic assessment.

Files:
- social_media_posts.csv: 120 synthetic posts with timestamps, accounts and interaction relationships.
- source_register.csv: source metadata.
- media_register.csv: eight media artifacts and investigation hints.
- source_documents/: selected textual evidence.
- media_evidence/: descriptions/notes corresponding to the media register.

Important:
Students should not assume the assessment hints are conclusions. They must independently examine evidence and justify their findings.
The example. invalid URLs are deliberately non-operational placeholders.
